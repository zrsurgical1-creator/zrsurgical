Modular Operation Theatres

Muhammadi Enterprises now delivers complete turnkey Modular Operation Theatre solutions — engineered for infection control, surgical precision, and long-term reliability. From single OT rooms to multi-suite complexes, we handle design, supply, and installation.

Our modular OT systems meet international standards for cleanroom classification, positive pressure ventilation, and antimicrobial surface requirements — making them suitable for general surgery, orthopedics, cardiac, and neurosurgery environments.
